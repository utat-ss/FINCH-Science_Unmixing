`simpler_data_rwc.csv` is lab data the majority of our work was done on, before modelling team provided their data. 

`useful_columns_data.csv` is a slightly cleaner version of the lab data, with extra columns to indicate whether there are endmembers.

`isprs_26_raw.csv` is data that was supplied to us by the modelling team.
and `isprs_26_new.csv` is the same data re-formated into a more familiar format. Note that its wavelengths range only from 900nm - 1690nm (incraments of 10nm).

`isprs_26_only_vza_rows.csv` removes the first 26 or so entries which were not actually modeled.
